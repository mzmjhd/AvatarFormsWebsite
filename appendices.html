Appendices

Additional project information.

1. Creating Interview Sessions

From the home page, click Upload Questionnaire.

On the Create Questionnaire Page, paste in your Microsoft Forms or Google Forms links in the designated textbox.

The questions will be extracted and you will be able to add, edit, or delete them. Click Create Questionnaire to generate the interview session.

2. Starting an interview

From the home page, click on any questionnaire from the questionnaire list.

Once in the Questionnaire Detail page. Click on Continue to Avatar.

This is where you have your interview session.

3. Configure Avatar, Language, and Voice

Choose an avatar, language, and voice profile that matches your interview context. You can also check runtime messages in the console and adjust settings when needed.

4. Manage Questionnaires from Dashboard

Use the Dashboard to review available questionnaires, access controls, and adjust settings for active workflows.

5. View Responses

After interviews are completed, open the responses list to see all submitted entries for a questionnaire.

6. View Response Details

Select a response from the list to inspect its full details, including each recorded answer.

7. Add Custom Avatars

Use Avaturn to generate a custom avatar model. Make sure to select the Type-2 model.

Customise the avatar appearance until it matches your preference.

Download the final avatar with animation for use in the application.

Click Browse in Settings to locate the downloaded avatar files on your device.

Upload Avatar to import the selected files into AvatarForms.

Select Avatar to make your newly imported model active for interviews.

GDPR Statement

This section describes how AvatarForms handles personal data under the UK GDPR. For project use, AvatarForms operates primarily as a Data Processor, processing form response data on behalf of the form owner/controller.

1. Scope of Processing

AvatarForms processes questionnaire response data entered during interviews. The exact personal data categories depend on the content of each source form (for example, free-text responses and selected options).

2. Local-Only Processing

All processing is performed locally on the user's device. Data does not leave the running device as part of normal operation. Local AI components and frameworks (including Llamafile, OpenVINO, and local TTS tooling) are used without cloud transfer by default.

3. Storage and Retention

Data is stored locally in project/application files and retained until manual deletion by the user or administrator. No fixed automatic retention period is enforced in the current implementation.

4. International Transfers and Third Parties

No routine transfers outside the UK/EU are intended in the local configuration. No external cloud analytics or hosted LLM service is required for core operation.

5. Data Subject Rights

Where applicable, data subjects may exercise their UK GDPR rights, including:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object

6. Contact

For data protection queries or rights requests, contact: muaz.mujahid.24@ucl.ac.uk

You may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe personal data has been handled unlawfully.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AvatarForms handles personal data for end users of the application.

1. What Data We Process

AvatarForms processes form and interview response content provided by users. The exact data depends on the form questions configured by the form owner/controller.

2. Lawful Basis

Processing is based on consent. By proceeding with data entry and interview submission, users consent to processing of their response data for the intended form purpose.

3. How Data Is Processed

Data is processed locally on the device running AvatarForms. Local AI components may be used to support interview and response workflows. Under the standard local setup, data is not sent to external cloud services.

4. Storage and Retention

Data is stored locally in application/project files and retained until manually deleted by the user or administrator.

5. Cookies, Tracking, and Telemetry

AvatarForms does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics telemetry in its standard local configuration.

6. Children and Sensitive Data

AvatarForms is not intended for children without appropriate supervision and legal basis. Special category data (for example health, biometric, or other sensitive personal data) should not be collected unless explicitly required and lawfully managed by the responsible controller.

7. Your Rights

Where applicable, users can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection in line with UK GDPR rights.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards for a local-first application setup. However, no method of storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact: muaz.mujahid.24@ucl.ac.uk
