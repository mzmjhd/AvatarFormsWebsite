Summary of Achievements

Our implementation outcomes aligned well with the MoSCoW requirements defined in the project. The key Must Have goals were achieved by delivering guided prompt rephrasing with follow-up clarification, support for multiple form input types, multilingual interaction support, and profile-aware user flows. We also addressed Should Have priorities by providing multiple avatar options and shaping the platform to support practical deployment scenarios. Could Have features, including sign language support, Microsoft Teams integration, and ultra-realistic avatars, were identified as valuable extensions for future iterations rather than blockers for a usable release. Across all stages, we maintained the Will not commitments by treating privacy protection and fairness across user groups as non-negotiable constraints.
